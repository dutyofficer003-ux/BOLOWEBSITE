Intelligence Secrecy

Welcome to the Intelligence Secrecy protocol hub. Elite members are bound by strict non-disclosure agreements regarding all organizational operations and intelligence.

Classification Levels

Information within the organization is strictly compartmentalized. We utilize a multi-tiered classification system ensuring that members only have access to data necessary for their specific operational tier. Attempting to bypass these classification levels or requesting intelligence above your clearance is a direct violation of conduct.

Secure Communication Handling

All sensitive information must be transmitted exclusively through our encrypted communication channels. The use of public networks, unverified third-party messaging apps, or personal email accounts to discuss organizational matters is strictly prohibited. Members are required to use approved devices with mandatory security software installed.

Consequences of Information Leaks

The organization exercises zero tolerance for information leaks. Any unauthorized disclosure of classified intelligence, member identities, or operational tactics will result in immediate termination of Elite status. Furthermore, members responsible for breaches are subject to severe legal consequences, asset freezing, and global blacklisting across all affiliated networks.
