Attendance Requirements

Attendance at all mandatory events is required for continued Elite membership. Failure to attend without prior approval from the Secretary General may result in suspension of privileges and review of membership status. Members must provide minimum 30 days notice for excused absences, which are granted only for critical personal or professional obligations.
