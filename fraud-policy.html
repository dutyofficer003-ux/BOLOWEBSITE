Offenses and Deceptions

Strict policies governing prohibited activities and breach consequences

Prohibited Activities

  • 🚫
    Third-Party Payments: Processing financial transactions through unauthorized agents or external payment systems
  • 🚫
    Information Disclosure: Sharing classified intelligence with unauthorized individuals or entities
  • 🚫
    Protocol Violation: Bypassing or circumventing established security and communication protocols
  • 🚫
    Fraudulent Activities: Engaging in deceptive practices or misrepresentation of authority
  • 🚫
    Unauthorized Access: Attempting to access systems or data beyond clearance level

Breach Consequences

  • ⚖️
    Immediate Suspension: All access privileges and benefits revoked without notice
  • 📋
    Financial Penalties: Forfeiture of all contributions and accumulated benefits
  • 🚷
    Legal Action: Pursuit of all available legal remedies and damages
  • 🔇
    Permanent Disqualification: Ineligible for future membership consideration

Zero Tolerance Policy

Any violation of these policies will result in immediate and permanent termination of membership status, with no possibility of appeal or reinstatement.
