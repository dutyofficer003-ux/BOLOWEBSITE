Financial Protocol

Wealth quotas, withdrawal thresholds, and account management for Elite members

Withdrawal Threshold

$500,000 USD

Elite Members must reach this total account balance before their first 'Redrawer' (Withdrawal) is authorized.

Monthly Load

  • 📅
    Architect's Provision: Automatic monthly credit of $50,000 USD loaded into active Elite accounts.
  • ⏰
    Load Schedule: Credits are processed on the 1st of each month at 00:00 UTC.
  • 🔄
    Compounding: Monthly loads compound with existing balance to accelerate threshold achievement.
  • 📊
    Performance Tracking: All monthly loads are logged and tracked for compliance reporting.

Annual Quota

  • 💰
    Mandatory Contribution: Required annual contribution to maintain the flow of protection and wealth.
  • 🎯
    Tier-Based Amount: Quota amounts determined by membership tier and influence level.
  • 📈
    Wealth Circulation: Contributions fund the pyramid logic and ensure continuous capital flow.
  • 🛡️
    Protection Maintenance: Quota payments activate and sustain Oversight Protection protocols.
